# RBI Master Circular: Prudential Norms on Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning (IRACP)

**Regulatory Reference:** RBI/2023-24/06 DOR.STR.REC.3/21.04.048/2023-24  
**Subject:** Non-Performing Assets (NPAs), SMA Framework, and Loss Provisioning  
**Applicability:** All Commercial Banks in India  

---

## 1. Non-Performing Asset (NPA) Core Classification Rules

An asset, including a lease asset, becomes non-performing when it ceases to generate income for the bank. An account is tagged as an NPA if:

1. **Term Loans:** Interest and/or instalment of principal remains overdue for a period of **more than 90 days**.
2. **Overdraft / Cash Credit (OD/CC):** The account remains "out of order" for a period of more than 90 days:
   - Outstanding balance remains continuously in excess of the sanctioned limit/drawing power, OR
   - No credits are deposited into the account for 90 days, or credits are insufficient to cover the interest debited during the period.
3. **Bills Purchased / Discounted:** The bill remains overdue and unpaid for a period of **more than 90 days**.
4. **Agricultural Loans:**
   - Short-duration crops: Overdue for **two crop seasons**.
   - Long-duration crops: Overdue for **one crop season**.

### Mandatory Daily Stamping Rule (RBI Mandate)
Banks are required to evaluate delinquency and classify loan accounts on a **daily end-of-day (EOD) process**, rather than month-end or quarter-end batch runs.

---

## 2. Early Warning Framework: Special Mention Accounts (SMA)

Before an account degrades to NPA status, banks must identify stress based on the number of Days Past Due (DPD):

| SMA Sub-Category | Basis for Classification (Loans other than revolving facilities) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SMA-0** | Principal or interest payment not fully overdue for more than 30 days, but showing signs of incipient stress |
| **SMA-1** | Principal or interest overdue between **31 days and 60 days** |
| **SMA-2** | Principal or interest overdue between **61 days and 90 days** |

*CRILC Reporting:* All borrower exposures of ₹5 Crore and above tagged as SMA-2 must be reported immediately to the Central Repository of Information on Large Credits (CRILC).

---

## 3. Four-Tier Asset Classification Hierarchy

Once an account becomes an NPA, it is categorized into one of three non-performing tiers based on the duration of delinquency:

```
  Standard Asset (Performing: 0 to 90 DPD)
       │
       ▼ (Day 91)
  Substandard Asset (NPA for <= 12 months)
       │
       ▼ (After 12 months of substandard status)
  Doubtful Asset (D1 -> D2 -> D3)
       │
       ▼ (Identified as uncollectible)
  Loss Asset (Zero salvage value)
```

1. **Standard Asset:** Generates regular interest and principal payments; poses no unusual risk.
2. **Substandard Asset:** An asset that has remained an NPA for a period of **less than or equal to 12 months**.
3. **Doubtful Asset:** An asset that has remained in the substandard category for a period of **more than 12 months**:
   - **Doubtful 1 (D1):** Delinquent for up to 1 year in doubtful status.
   - **Doubtful 2 (D2):** Delinquent for 1 to 3 years in doubtful status.
   - **Doubtful 3 (D3):** Delinquent for more than 3 years in doubtful status.
4. **Loss Asset:** An asset where loss has been identified by the bank, internal/external auditors, or RBI inspection, but the amount has not been written off wholly. It is considered uncollectible.

---

## 4. Minimum Provisioning Norms

Banks must set aside capital reserves (provisions) from their operating profits against potential loan losses:

### 4.1 Standard Asset Provisions
- Direct advances to Agriculture and SME sectors: **0.25%**
- Residential housing loans up to ₹30 Lakh: **0.25%**
- Commercial Real Estate - Residential Housing (CRE-RH): **0.75%**
- Commercial Real Estate (general): **1.00%**
- All other standard retail and corporate loans: **0.40%**

### 4.2 Non-Performing Asset Provisions

| Asset Category | Secured Portion Provision | Unsecured Portion Provision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Substandard Asset** | **15%** | **25%** (or 20% for infrastructure) |
| **Doubtful 1 (D1 - up to 1 year)** | **25%** | **100%** |
| **Doubtful 2 (D2 - 1 to 3 years)**| **40%** | **100%** |
| **Doubtful 3 (D3 - > 3 years)** | **100%** | **100%** |
| **Loss Asset** | **100%** | **100%** (Must be 100% written off or provided) |

---

## 5. Income Recognition & Upgradation Norms

1. **Cessation of Accrual:** Once an account turns NPA, banks **must immediately stop recognizing interest income on an accrual basis**. Interest can only be accounted for when actually received in cash.
2. **Upgradation Rule:** A loan account classified as NPA can **only be upgraded to Standard category if all arrears of interest and principal are fully paid** by the borrower. Partial repayments cannot restore standard status.
