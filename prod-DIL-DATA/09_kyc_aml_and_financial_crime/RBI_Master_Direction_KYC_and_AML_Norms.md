# RBI Master Direction: Know Your Customer (KYC) & Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

**Regulatory Reference:** RBI/DBR/2015-16/18 Master Direction DBR.AML.BC.No.81/14.01.001/2015-16  
**Statutory Basis:** Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002 & PML Rules, 2005  
**Applicability:** All Regulated Entities (REs) in India  

---

## 1. Customer Identification: Officially Valid Documents (OVD)

Under PML Rule 2(1)(d), no banking account can be opened without obtaining proof of identity and permanent address through one of the designated **Officially Valid Documents (OVDs)**:

1. **Passport**
2. **Driving Licence** (smart card or digitized)
3. **Proof of possession of Aadhaar number** (with offline verification or voluntary masked e-KYC)
4. **Voter's Identity Card** issued by the Election Commission of India
5. **Job card issued by NREGA** signed by an officer of the State Government
6. **Letter issued by the National Population Register (NPR)** containing details of name and address

*Deemed OVDs for Address (if OVD doesn't contain current address):*  
Utility bill (electricity, telephone, post-paid mobile, piped gas, water bill) not older than **two months**, property or municipal tax receipt, or pension payment orders. (Customer must submit updated OVD within 3 months).

---

## 2. Customer Risk Categorization & Periodic Review Schedules

Regulated entities must categorize all customers into **Low, Medium, and High Risk** profiles based on parameters such as customer identity, social/financial status, nature of business activity, geographic location, and turnover.

| Risk Category | Customer Profiles (Examples) | Mandatory KYC Refresh Frequency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High Risk** | Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs), Non-Resident customers, high net-worth individuals, trusts, charities, NGOs, jewellers, arms dealers, cash-intensive businesses | **Every 2 Years** |
| **Medium Risk** | Salaried employees with high transaction volumes, small businesses, partnership firms, rental income earners | **Every 8 Years** |
| **Low Risk** | Salaried individuals (well-reputed employers), pensioners, government departments, basic savings bank deposit (BSBD) account holders | **Every 10 Years** |

---

## 3. Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) Norms

1. **Definition:** Individuals who are or have been entrusted with prominent public functions in a foreign country (e.g., Heads of State/Government, senior politicians, senior government/judicial/military officers, senior executives of state-owned corporations).
2. **Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD):**
   - Gathering approval from a **Senior Management official** before establishing a banking relationship with a PEP or their family members/close associates.
   - Verifying the source of funds and source of wealth conclusively.
   - Subject to continuous enhanced transaction monitoring.

## 4. Statutory Reporting to FIU-IND (PMLA Section 12)

All banks must appoint a **Designated Director** and a **Principal Officer** to report financial crime data to the Financial Intelligence Unit - India (FIU-IND):

| Report Type | Trigger Condition | Filing Deadline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cash Transaction Report (CTR)** | All cash transactions of value more than **₹10 Lakh** (or equivalent foreign currency), or series of integrally connected cash transactions exceeding ₹10L in a calendar month | By **15th day of the succeeding month** |
| **Suspicious Transaction Report (STR)** | Any transaction where there is reasonable ground of suspicion regarding criminal proceeds, terror financing, or lack of economic rationale | Not later than **7 working days** after forming suspicion |
| **Non-Profit Organization (NTPR)** | All cash transactions involving receipts by non-profit organizations exceeding ₹10 Lakh | By **15th day of the succeeding month** |
| **Counterfeit Currency Report (CCR)**| Detection of forged or counterfeit currency notes across teller counters | By **15th day of the succeeding month** |

### Anti-Tipping Off Provision (Rule 8(3))
Banks, their directors, officers, and employees are **strictly prohibited from disclosing or "tipping off"** to the customer or any third party that an STR or suspicious inquiry has been initiated or reported to FIU-IND.

## 5. Income Tax Rule 114B: Mandatory Quoting of PAN

Permanent Account Number (PAN) or Form No. 60 must be mandatorily quoted for:
- Opening an account (other than a Basic Savings Bank Deposit account).
- A deposit of cash exceeding **₹50,000** in a single day.
- A purchase of bank drafts, pay orders, or banker's cheques in cash exceeding **₹50,000** in a day.
- Cash deposits or withdrawals aggregating to **₹20 Lakh or more** in a financial year.
