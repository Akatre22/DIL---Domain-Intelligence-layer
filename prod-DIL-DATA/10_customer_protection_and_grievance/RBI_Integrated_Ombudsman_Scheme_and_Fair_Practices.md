# Consumer Protection: Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme & Fair Practices Code

**Regulatory Scheme:** Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS), 2021  
**Statutory Backing:** Section 35A of Banking Regulation Act, 1949 & Section 45L of RBI Act, 1934  

---

## 1. The Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (One Nation - One Ombudsman)

Effective November 12, 2021, the RBI unified three previous ombudsman systems (Banking Ombudsman, NBFC Ombudsman, and Digital Transactions Ombudsman) into a single centralized portal under the **Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre (CRPC)** in Chandigarh.

### 1.1 Escalation Hierarchy & Timelines
```
  Step 1: Customer lodges complaint with Bank's Internal Grievance Redressal (GRO)
       │
       ▼ (Wait period: 30 days)
  Step 2: If no response in 30 days OR customer dissatisfied with response:
       │
       ▼
  Step 3: File complaint with RBI Integrated Ombudsman (within 1 year of rejection)
       │
       ▼
  Step 4: Conciliation / Mediation or Ombudsman Award
       │
       ▼ (If dissatisfied with award)
  Step 5: Appeal to Appellate Authority (Executive Director, RBI) within 30 days
```

### 1.2 Compensation Award Limits
- **Direct Financial Compensation:** The Ombudsman has the power to award compensation up to **₹20 Lakh (₹2,000,000)** for actual loss suffered by the complainant.
- **Mental Agony / Harassment / Loss of Time:** The Ombudsman may award an additional compensation of up to **₹1 Lakh (₹100,000)** to the complainant for harassment, mental distress, and expense incurred.

## 2. Fair Practices Code for Lenders

Mandated standards governing how banks treat credit applicants and borrowers:

1. **Loan Application Acknowledgment:** Every loan application must be formally acknowledged with an indicative time-frame within which the bank will convey its sanction or rejection.
2. **Transparent Sanction Letters:**
   - Must specify the exact Annualized Rate of Interest, penal interest rate for delayed repayment, and all fees/charges.
   - The borrower must sign and return a duplicate copy of the sanction letter, which forms an integral contract.
3. **Penal Charges vs. Penal Interest (RBI Master Direction Jan 2024):**
   - Banks are **prohibited from levying penal interest** (compounding penalty into principal/interest rates).
   - Penalties for delayed payment can only be levied as **reasonable, transparent "Penal Charges"** and must not be capitalized (no interest computed on penal charges).
4. **Timely Release of Collateral Documents:**
   - Upon full repayment and closure of a loan account, banks **must release all original movable/immovable property documents within 30 calendar days**.
   - *Penalty for Delay:* If a bank fails to return documents within 30 days, it must compensate the borrower at **₹5,000 per day of delay**.
