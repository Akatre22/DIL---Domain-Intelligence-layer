## 2. Retail Loan Underwriting Standards

### 2.1 Home Loans (Housing Finance)
1. **Loan-to-Value (LTV) Statutorily Capped Rules:**
   - Ticket Size $\le$ ₹30 Lakh: Maximum LTV **90%** (Requires minimum 10% borrower equity contribution).
   - Ticket Size > ₹30 Lakh and $\le$ ₹75 Lakh: Maximum LTV **80%** (Minimum 20% down payment).
   - Ticket Size > ₹75 Lakh: Maximum LTV **75%** (Minimum 25% down payment).
   - *Prohibition:* Registration charges, stamp duty, and documentation fees **cannot** be capitalized into the loan amount if the LTV exceeds statutory limits.
2. **Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR / DTI):**
   - Net Monthly Income < ₹50,000: FOIR ceiling is **50%**.
   - Net Monthly Income ₹50,000 to ₹1,50,000: FOIR ceiling is **60%**.
   - Net Monthly Income > ₹1,50,000: FOIR ceiling is **65%**.
3. **Legal & Technical Verification:**
   - Legal Search Report (LSR) across a minimum 13-year (preferably 30-year) title chain.
   - Independent valuation from two empaneled structural engineers if loan value exceeds ₹1 Crore.

### 2.2 Gold Loans (Ornament Collateral)
1. **Statutory LTV Ceiling:** Per RBI circular, the loan amount shall not exceed **75% of the intrinsic value of gold** (calculated as average closing price of 22-carat gold over past 30 days). No charges or interest can cause LTV to breach 75% at origination.
2. **Hallmarking & Purity:** Accepted purity: 18K to 24K gold ornaments only. Gold bars and coins cannot exceed 50 grams per customer.
3. **Repayment Structures:**
   - Standard Monthly EMI.
   - Bullet Repayment: Both principal and interest are payable at the end of the 12-month tenure.

### 2.3 Personal Loans (Unsecured Lending)
1. **Employment & Income Criteria:**
   - Minimum verified continuous employment of 12 months with current employer (24 months total work experience).
   - Category A corporate employees: Minimum net monthly salary ₹25,000.
   - Category B/C corporate or SME employees: Minimum net monthly salary ₹35,000.
2. **Maximum Exposure:** Capped at 15 to 20 times net monthly income, subject to a maximum unsecured retail ticket of ₹40 Lakh.
3. **Bureau Checks:** Zero instances of 30+ Days Past Due (DPD) across any credit card or unsecured loan within the preceding 12 months.

### 2.4 Education Loans
1. **Margin Money Requirements:**
   - Loans up to ₹4 Lakh: **0% Margin** (100% financing).
   - Loans > ₹4 Lakh for studies in India: **5% Margin**.
   - Loans > ₹4 Lakh for studies abroad: **15% Margin**.
2. **Collateral & Security Norms:**
   - Up to ₹4 Lakh: No collateral, parent/guardian as co-borrower.
   - Above ₹4 Lakh to ₹7.5 Lakh: Third-party personal guarantee acceptable in lieu of collateral.
   - Above ₹7.5 Lakh: Tangible collateral security (property, fixed deposit, NSC) of 100% value required.
3. **Moratorium Period:** Course duration plus 1 year (or 6 months after obtaining employment, whichever is earlier).

### 2.5 Credit Cards
1. **Credit Limit Assignment:** Standard underwriting formula assigns 2.5x to 3x net monthly salary for salaried individuals.
2. **Minimum Amount Due (MAD):** Standard 5% of the total billed amount or aggregate of all finance charges, fees, and taxes plus 100% of over-limit amounts.
3. **Most Important Terms & Conditions (MITC):**
   - Annualized Percentage Rate (APR) disclosure mandated in bold on every monthly statement.
   - Prohibition on unrequested card issuance or automated credit limit expansion without explicit opt-in.
