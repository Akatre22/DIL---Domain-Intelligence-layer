# SME & Commercial Lending: Working Capital Assessment & Government Guarantee Schemes

**Category:** SME / MSME Credit & Commercial Facilities  
**Regulatory Framework:** RBI Master Direction - Lending to Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)  

---

## 1. MSME Classification Criteria (Government of India / RBI Definition)

Per notification effective July 1, 2020, MSMEs are categorized uniformly across manufacturing and service sectors based on composite criteria of **Plant & Machinery / Equipment Investment** and **Annual Turnover**:

| Category | Investment in Plant & Machinery / Equipment | Annual Turnover Limit (Excluding Exports) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Micro Enterprise** | Does not exceed **₹1 Crore** | Does not exceed **₹5 Crore** |
| **Small Enterprise** | Does not exceed **₹10 Crore** | Does not exceed **₹50 Crore** |
| **Medium Enterprise** | Does not exceed **₹50 Crore** | Does not exceed **₹250 Crore** |

## 2. Working Capital Assessment Methodologies

Banks utilize two standard calculation methods to determine the Maximum Permissible Bank Finance (MPBF) for SME working capital:

### 2.1 Turnover Method (Nayak Committee Norms)
*Applicability:* Small and Micro enterprises with aggregate fund-based working capital requirements **up to ₹5 Crore**.
- **Working Capital Requirement:** Assessed as **25%** of projected annual gross sales turnover.
- **Promoter / Borrower Margin:** Borrower must contribute at least **5%** of projected turnover as Net Working Capital (margin).
- **Bank Working Capital Sanction (MPBF):** Capped at **20%** of projected annual turnover:
  $$\text{Working Capital Limit} = \text{Projected Annual Turnover} \times 20\%$$

### 2.2 Traditional MPBF Method (Tandon Committee Second Method)
*Applicability:* Medium enterprises and corporate borrowers with working capital limits **exceeding ₹5 Crore**.
- Current Assets (CA) are evaluated alongside Current Liabilities (CL) excluding bank borrowings.
- **Borrower Contribution:** Borrower must fund at least **25% of Total Current Assets** out of long-term funds (equity / net working capital).
- **Bank Finance Calculation:**
  $$\text{MPBF} = (0.75 \times \text{Current Assets}) - \text{Current Liabilities (non-bank)}$$
- Requires maintaining a minimum **Current Ratio of 1.33:1**.

## 3. Key Government Credit Guarantee Schemes

### 3.1 CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises)
- **Objective:** Collateral-free credit facilities to new and existing Micro and Small enterprises.
- **Maximum Loan Eligible:** Up to **₹5 Crore** per borrower.
- **Guarantee Cover:**
  - Standard Micro enterprises: Up to **85%** for loans up to ₹5 Lakh.
  - Standard credits up to ₹5 Crore: **75%** of the default amount.
  - Women-owned MSMEs, ZED-certified units: Up to **85%**.
- **No Third-Party Collateral:** Banks are prohibited from accepting collateral or third-party guarantees for facilities covered under CGTMSE.

### 3.2 Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)
Collateral-free micro loans categorized into three developmental stages:
1. **Shishu:** Loans up to **₹50,000** (for startups/micro businesses).
2. **Kishor:** Loans above **₹50,000 and up to ₹5,00,000** (for purchasing equipment/inventory).
3. **Tarun:** Loans above **₹5,00,000 and up to ₹10,00,000** (or ₹20,00,000 under Tarun Plus for successful track record units).
