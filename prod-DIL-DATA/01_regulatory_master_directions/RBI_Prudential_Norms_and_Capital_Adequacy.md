# RBI Master Direction: Prudential Framework for Capital Adequacy & Risk Management (Basel III)

**Authority:** Reserve Bank of India (Department of Regulation)  
**Applicability:** All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding Small Finance Banks and Payments Banks)  
**Regulatory Reference:** RBI/2015-16/58 DBR.No.BP.BC.1/21.06.201/2015-16  

---

## 1. Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR / CRAR) Requirements

Under RBI Basel III implementation guidelines, commercial banks in India are required to maintain a higher minimum capital threshold than the global Basel baseline of 8.0%.

| Capital Component | Minimum Regulatory Ratio (as % of Risk Weighted Assets - RWAs) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1)** | **5.50%** |
| **Tier 1 Capital (CET1 + Additional Tier 1)** | **7.00%** |
| **Total Capital (Tier 1 + Tier 2)** | **9.00%** |
| **Capital Conservation Buffer (CCB)** | **2.50%** (comprising common equity) |
| **Minimum Total Capital + CCB** | **11.50%** |

### Definitions:
- **Tier 1 Capital (Going-Concern Capital):** Absorbs losses while the bank continues operations. Primarily includes common shares, statutory reserves, share premium, and capital reserves minus intangible assets and goodwill.
- **Tier 2 Capital (Gone-Concern Capital):** Subordinated debt instruments and general loan loss provisions (up to a ceiling of 1.25% of credit risk-weighted assets).

## 2. Risk Weights on Credit Exposures

Risk-weighted assets (RWAs) determine how much capital a bank must hold against each rupee lent. Higher risk loans require higher capital allocation.

### 2.1 Residential Housing Loans (Individual Borrowers)
Risk weights for housing loans are strictly linked to the **Loan-to-Value (LTV)** ratio and ticket size:

| Ticket Size | LTV Ratio Range | Risk Weight (%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Up to ₹30 Lakh | $\le 80\%$ | **35%** |
| Up to ₹30 Lakh | $> 80\%$ and $\le 90\%$ | **50%** |
| Above ₹30 Lakh to ₹75 Lakh | $\le 80\%$ | **35%** |
| Above ₹75 Lakh | $\le 75\%$ | **50%** |

*Note:* Housing loans with LTV exceeding 90% (for loans $\le$ ₹30L) or exceeding 80% (for loans $\le$ ₹75L) are prohibited under statutory directives.

### 2.2 Consumer Credit, Personal Loans & Credit Cards
- **Credit Card Receivables:** Risk weight of **150%**.
- **Personal Unsecured Retail Loans:** Risk weight of **125%** (revised upward by RBI in Nov 2023 from 100% to curb unsecured lending growth).
- **Vehicle Loans & Gold Loans:** Risk weight of **100%**.

### 2.3 Commercial Real Estate (CRE)
- Commercial Real Estate - Residential Housing (CRE-RH): **75%**.
- Other Commercial Real Estate (Malls, Commercial Offices, Land Banking): **100% to 150%**.

## 3. Large Exposure Framework (LEF)

To avoid credit concentration risk, RBI mandates strict prudential limits on exposure to single and connected counterparties:

1. **Single Counterparty Limit:** Total exposure (fund-based and non-fund-based) must not exceed **20%** of the bank's eligible capital base (Tier 1 Capital). Under exceptional circumstances approved by the bank's Board, an additional 5% may be allowed.
2. **Group of Connected Counterparties Limit:** Total exposure to a group of economically interlinked corporate entities must not exceed **25%** of the bank's Tier 1 Capital.
3. **Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs):** Bank exposure to a single privately-owned NBFC is capped at **20%** of Tier 1 capital (or 25% if lending to infrastructure finance NBFCs).

## 4. Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) & Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)

- **Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR):** Mandates banks to maintain a buffer of unencumbered **High-Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA)** (cash, central bank reserves, government securities) equal to or exceeding **100%** of expected total net cash outflows over a 30-day stressed scenario:
  $$\text{LCR} = \frac{\text{Stock of High Quality Liquid Assets}}{\text{Total Net Cash Outflows over 30 Days}} \ge 100\%$$
- **Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR):** Promotes long-term resilience by requiring banks to fund their activities with sufficiently stable sources of funding over a 1-year horizon:
  $$\text{NSFR} = \frac{\text{Available Stable Funding (ASF)}}{\text{Required Stable Funding (RSF)}} \ge 100\%$$
