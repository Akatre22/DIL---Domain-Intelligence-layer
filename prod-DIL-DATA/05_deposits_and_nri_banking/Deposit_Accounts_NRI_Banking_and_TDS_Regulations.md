# Comprehensive Guide: Deposit Accounts, NRI Banking & Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)

**Regulatory Reference:** RBI Master Direction - Interest Rate on Deposits & FEMA Regulations  
**Insurance Coverage:** Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) Act, 1961  

---

## 1. Domestic Deposit Accounts: Operating Norms

### 1.1 Savings & Current Accounts
- **Savings Bank Account:** Available only to individuals, HUFs, and approved non-profit entities. Operating companies and commercial businesses cannot maintain savings accounts.
- **Current Account:** Intended for commercial transactions. Governed by RBI's **Current Account Opening Circular (2020/2021)**:
  - If a borrower has aggregate credit facilities $\ge$ ₹50 Crore from the banking system, only the **escrow-managing bank** or designated lender can maintain the primary collection current account.
  - Lending banks must monitor credits to prevent fund diversion outside consortium exposure.

### 1.2 Fixed / Term Deposits (FD / TD)
- **Tenure:** Minimum tenure of **7 days** up to a maximum of **10 years**.
- **Premature Withdrawal:** Permitted unless explicitly opened under the non-callable deposit variant (typically available for deposits $\ge$ ₹1 Crore). Standard penalty for early closure is 0.50% to 1.00% reduction from the contracted interest rate.
- **Senior Citizen Additional Interest:** Lenders customarily offer an additional 50 basis points (0.50%) per annum on term deposits of resident individuals aged 60 years and above.

### 1.3 Statutory Deposit Insurance (DICGC)
- Under the DICGC (Amendment) Act 2021, deposits across all branches of each insured commercial bank are insured up to a **maximum ceiling of ₹5,00,000 (₹5 Lakh)** per depositor.
- Covers both principal and accrued interest combined across all savings, current, recurring, and fixed deposit accounts in the same capacity and same right.

## 2. Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under Section 194A of Income Tax Act

Banks must deduct tax at source on aggregate interest earned across all term deposits in a financial year:

| Depositor Category | Annual Interest Threshold for TDS | Standard TDS Rate (with PAN) | TDS Rate (without PAN / Invalid PAN) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Regular Resident Individuals / Corporates** | **₹40,000** | **10%** | **20%** (Section 206AA) |
| **Resident Senior Citizens (Age $\ge$ 60)** | **₹50,000** | **10%** | **20%** |

*Form 15G / 15H Exemption:* Depositors whose estimated total income is below the taxable threshold can submit **Form 15G** (under 60 years) or **Form 15H** (senior citizens) to receive interest payments without tax deduction.

## 3. NRI Banking Accounts (Non-Resident Indian Framework)

Under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) are strictly prohibited from holding regular domestic resident accounts. They must convert or open specialized NRI accounts:

| Feature | NRE Account (Non-Resident External) | NRO Account (Non-Resident Ordinary) | FCNR(B) Account (Foreign Currency Non-Resident) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Denomination Currency** | Indian Rupee (INR) | Indian Rupee (INR) | Designated Foreign Currency (USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, CAD, AUD) |
| **Source of Funds** | Foreign inward remittances or transfer from other NRE/FCNR accounts | Legitimate domestic earnings in India (rent, dividends, pension, salary) | Foreign inward remittances in designated convertible currencies |
| **Repatriability** | **Freely and fully repatriable** (principal + interest) | **Restricted repatriation** (up to USD 1 Million per financial year via Form 15CA/CB) | **Freely and fully repatriable** (zero exchange rate risk) |
| **Tax Treatment in India** | **100% Tax-Free** (Interest is fully exempt from Indian Income Tax) | **Taxable in India** (Standard 30% TDS + applicable surcharge and cess, subject to DTAA) | **100% Tax-Free** (Interest is completely tax-exempt) |
| **Permitted Account Types**| Savings, Current, Recurring, Fixed Deposit | Savings, Current, Recurring, Fixed Deposit | **Term Deposit Only** (Tenure: 1 year to 5 years) |
