# Corporate Banking: Consortium Lending, Syndicated Facilities & Project Finance

**Category:** Wholesale Banking & Structured Finance  
**Regulatory Standards:** RBI Framework on Resolution of Stressed Assets & Large Exposures  

---

## 1. Consortium vs. Multiple Banking vs. Syndication

When a corporate borrower requires substantial debt capital exceeding the single lending limit of a single bank, funding is structured via multi-lender arrangements:

| Dimension | Consortium Lending | Multiple Banking Arrangement (MBA) | Syndicated Loan |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Documentation** | Single common loan agreement & Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA) | Separate independent loan agreements with each individual lender | Single syndicated credit agreement via Lead Arranger / Facility Agent |
| **Security & Charge** | Pari-passu first charge shared via Security Trustee | Individual separate charges or difficult second charges | Single charge created in favor of the Facility Security Trustee |
| **Lead Bank Role** | Lead bank assesses total credit requirement and coordinates covenants | No single coordinator; lenders independently assess risk | Lead Arranger underwrites or markets the loan facility |
| **Escrow / TRA** | Mandated single Trust & Retention Account (TRA) | Dispersed cash flows, high risk of fund diversion | Mandated centralized proceeds account managed by Agent |

## 2. Project Finance & Debt Covenants

For long-term capital infrastructure, industrial plants, energy, and toll road financing:

### 2.1 Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
The most critical solvency metric for project cash flows:
$$\text{DSCR} = \frac{\text{Net Operating Income after Tax} + \text{Depreciation} + \text{Interest on Term Debt}}{\text{Annual Principal Debt Repayment} + \text{Annual Interest Payment}}$$
- **Standard Infrastructure Minimum:** Minimum average DSCR of **1.20:1 to 1.35:1**.
- **Commercial Projects Minimum:** Average DSCR of **1.50:1**.

### 2.2 Date of Commencement of Commercial Operations (DCCO)
- **Financial Closure:** Obligation that all equity commitments and debt agreements are signed before first disbursement.
- **Deemed Deferment Norms:** RBI allows deferment of DCCO up to **2 years** for infrastructure projects (and 1 year for non-infrastructure projects) without treating the account as restructured/NPA, provided the delay is due to reasons beyond the promoter's control (e.g., litigation or statutory clearances).

### 2.3 Trust and Retention Account (TRA) Mechanism
All project cash revenues are routed into a strictly monitored waterfall account:
1. *Tier 1:* Statutory dues and taxes.
2. *Tier 2:* Permitted monthly plant operating & maintenance (O&M) expenses.
3. *Tier 3:* Current debt service (Interest + Principal).
4. *Tier 4:* Transfer to Debt Service Reserve Account (DSRA - typically 1 to 2 quarters of debt service).
5. *Tier 5:* Residual cash flows released as promoter dividends (only if no covenants are breached).
